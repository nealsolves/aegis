# Regulated Overlay

## Profile type

This is an additive **overlay**, not a base profile. Apply it to solo, team, or
prototype only when the declared external obligations require regulated controls.
The overlay may override solo allowances and team defaults through deny-overrides.

## Required additions

- Maintain external control mapping from each applicable obligation to the
  implementation, evidence, owner, status, exception, and revalidation date.
- Define evidence retention periods, storage, access controls, integrity, and
  disposal from the governing source rather than a generic default.
- Name required approvers and their authority basis for affected changes,
  releases, production actions, exceptions, and residual risk.
- Enforce segregation when required. A true regulatory segregation constraint
  can override solo allowances and prohibit role collapse.
- Define exception authority and prohibited exceptions. Project policy cannot
  waive a legal, contractual, or regulatory boundary it does not own.
- Preserve auditability: exact actors, timestamps, policy/context/change hashes,
  decision sources, evidence references, changes, and revalidation.

## Claims boundary

This overlay does not claim certification, attestation, audit success, or legal
compliance. Template presence does not prove compliance. Only scoped, current,
externally supportable evidence can substantiate a status claimed elsewhere.

## Completion check

- Applicable obligations and control mappings are explicit.
- Retention, approvers, segregation, exception authority, and audit evidence are
  configured and more restrictive outcomes override base-profile allowances.
- Unsupported certification language is absent.
